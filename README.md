## AskGenie Legal

[AskGenie](https://askgenie.al) is a production legal workspace designed around the
daily work of Albanian practices. The live platform brings together:

- clients, matters, hearings, deadlines, tasks, documents and finance;
- JETA structured intake and guided professional workflows;
- a private client portal with a scoped link and separate six-digit access code;
- Albanian legislation and searchable court decisions;
- tenant isolation, roles, permissions, audit trails and controlled AI assistance.

The public website, authenticated workspace and client-facing flows now share one
clear product language: less manual work, one connected matter file and professional
review at every important step.
